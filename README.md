# PIGEON CAN'T STOP

### THE SNACKS MUST FLOW

A vertical, one-button rhythm-meme game starring the most insufferable pigeon in the city. Tap to peck snacks off a spinning disc. Keep the streak alive and the whole world speeds up 鈥?the platform, the pecks, the music, and the pigeon.

- **One-tap gameplay**: peck when the snack hits the arrow. Early, late, air, void, or a vegetable and it is over.
- **Combo escalation**: the tempo ramps with every streak, and the pigeon loses its mind in stages:
  - **x10 HEADBANG MODE** 鈥?the neck does the talking
  - **x20 SPIN CYCLE** 鈥?full rotational nonsense
  - **x30 DEAL WITH IT** 鈥?sunglasses deployed
  - **x50 LIFT OFF** 鈥?jetpack takeoff
  - **x100 PIGEON RAVE** 鈥?pure uncut dance
- **5 switchable scenes**, each with its own palette and visual language: ROOFTOP, NEON ALLEY, DISCO INFERNO, BRAINROT VOID, BUBBLEGUM PARK
- Random beat-synced meltdown events: wobble, zoomies, strobe, big bird, tiny snacks, snack rain
- Failure is entertainment: the pigeon freezes, slowly turns to judge you, and declares **BWARGH.**
- 100% single-file HTML5 鈥?zero dependencies, zero loaded files. All art is procedural Canvas 2D; every sound is synthesized live with Web Audio, including a music loop that accelerates with your combo and drops absurd vocal stabs at high streaks
- Phone (tap) and desktop (mouse / Space / Enter; keys 1-5 or arrows switch scenes); best combo saved locally
- A run lasts 30-60 seconds. Three seconds to learn.
